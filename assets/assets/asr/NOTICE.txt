Changes for this application: converted Hugging Face tensors to the OpenAI
Whisper layout; exported using sherpa-onnx's Whisper ONNX exporter; retained
the full-precision encoder after paired testing; rewrote the equivalent decoder output matrix product
to permit int8 quantization of MatMul and Gather weights. No vocabulary was
added, and no legal phrase recognition table is used.
For version 1.3.0, token byte fragments use lossless hexadecimal ASCII transport
through the native runtime and are decoded once after concatenation in Dart.
This prevents loss of Mongolian UTF-8 fragments; learned token IDs are unchanged.

Exporter: k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0), scripts/whisper/export-onnx.py.
Original copyright and source attribution remain in the retained exporter.
Runtime: sherpa_onnx 1.13.8 (Apache-2.0); microphone: record 6.2.0 (BSD-3-Clause).

The upstream model card reports 45.5102% word error rate on Common Voice 11
Mongolian. This is a major accuracy limitation, not an accuracy guarantee.
The converted model has not been validated for legal terms on physical phones.
Audio recognition is local; the app's separate MyMemory translation step
still needs internet and sends the recognized search text to MyMemory.
